On working together.

Tell me what you’re trying to do with the business.

I’ll look at it three ways. What your customers actually see. What you’ve been building toward. The ideas you keep coming back to but haven’t acted on.

My work reaches from figuring out how to explain the company to designing its identity, building its website, and working on the product itself. I help choose what needs attention and take responsibility for getting it made, including working with the people you already have.

AI is part of my daily work. I can also help you figure out where it would be useful in yours and get it working.

Sometimes there’s a particular project to carry through. Sometimes I stay involved as the business develops. Either way, you’ll be working directly with me.

Tell me about the idea you keep coming back to. That’s enough to begin.
